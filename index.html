Power Rankings

Score weights: 30% overall record, 30% record in the last 3 games, 10% overall points per game, 10% points per game in the last 3 games, and 20% quality Elo. Quality Elo rewards wins over above-average scoring opponents, penalizes losses to below-average scoring opponents, stays neutral for easy wins and quality losses, and slightly favors opponents with better records entering that game.
